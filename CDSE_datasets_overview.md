## Data Collections

### **Sentinel**

* Raw or pre-processed satellite imagery, governed by the EC/ESA Copernicus programme. Regular data collection for generic purposes, different satellite sensors complementing each other.
* Individual images or time series can be analyzed - the tool is the Custom script.
* Sentinel-2 True Color Istanbul: [link.dataspace.copernicus.eu/5b3v](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://link.dataspace.copernicus.eu/5b3v)

### **Copernicus Land Monitoring Service**

* The purpose of the Copernicus Programme is to support evidence-based policy --> *"The objective of Copernicus should be to provide accurate and reliable information in the field of the environment and security, tailored to the needs of users and supporting other Union policies, in particular relating to the internal market, transport, environment, energy, civil protection and civil security, cooperation with third countries and humanitarian aid."* (Regulation (EU) No 377/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 3 April 2014 establishing the Copernicus Programme)
* [https://land.copernicus.eu/en](https://land.copernicus.eu/en) --> DRD/FRD !

**Main Groups**

1. Bio-geophysical parameters (global/Europe)
2. Land cover and land use in priority areas (European cities)
3. Land cover and land use mapping (global/Europe)
